The Protocol

  • Warmup 1: 50% weight (auto-calculated).
  • Warmup 2: 75% weight (auto-calculated). The 2nd exercise for a muscle group skips Warmup 1 and does only this one.
  • Working Sets: 2 sets at 100% weight to Absolute Failure, dropping the weight on Set 2 if needed.
  • Rest: 3 minutes between sets. Tap Rest 3:00 at the bottom of any exercise.
  • Progression: Hit 8 reps? Add 5 lbs for next time (2.5 lbs on cable exercises).
  • Kettlebells: weight steps through standard bell sizes in kg, and warmups round to the nearest bell. Hit 8 reps? Go up one bell.
